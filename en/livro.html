Drop-dead Gorgeous Stories




Synopsis

As a palliative physician, Ana Claudia Quintana Arantes has been caring for terminal patients for more than twenty years, in intimate contact with the moments of greatest vulnerability of the human being.One of the main voices in the attempt to break the taboo about death in Brazil, she brings us a collection of exciting real stories collected in her daily practice, in which the proximity of the end reveals us in all our depth.

More about the author

Ana Claudia Quintana Arantes is a physician graduated from USP, with residency in Geriatrics and Gerontology at the Hospital das Clínicas of FMUSP. She has a postgraduate degree in Psychology – Interventions in Grief from the 4 Seasons Institute of Psychology and a specialization in Palliative Care from the Pallium Institute and the University of Oxford. She is a founding member of the Casa do Cuidar Association, where she coordinates the multiprofessional advanced training courses – Practice and Teaching in Palliative Care.

Books that address the topic



BOOK OF THE DEAD


The Ancient Egyptian Book of the Dead is one of the oldest and most influential texts in all of history. It is made up of a combination of prayers, spells, and speeches that the ancient Egyptians buried with their dead, with the aim of helping the deceased on their "journey" to the afterlife.



ABOUT DEATH AND DYING


Elisabeth Kübler-Ross describes in this work what has become a classic of the experiences of terminally ill patients, their agonies and frustrations, in an attempt to encourage people not to turn away from the condemned patients, but rather to draw near to them and help them in their last moments.



WE NEED TO TALK ABOUT DEATH


The purpose of this book is to allow people to become familiar with the process of dying. To this end, the stories were grouped by themes, starting with those that describe the process and evolution of death and the various ways people react to it.
