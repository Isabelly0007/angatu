Introduction


Welcome to our website dedicated to exploring the "Concept of Death Among Peoples." Death is a universal aspect of the human experience, however, the way it is understood and celebrated varies significantly between different cultures, historical periods and systems of connection. In this space, we will delve into the different perspectives on death in antiquity, in philosophy, among indigenous peoples, in art and religions around the world. Discover how death has been interpreted, ritualized and represented, and how these interpretations influence life and society. Join us on a journey of exploration and reflection on one of the most profound and universal themes of the human condition.
